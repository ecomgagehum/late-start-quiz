## The Free Quiz

Our free quiz at [quiz.confidenceofayes.com] asks you some quick questions and some open questions about your life, your week and dating. Here is what happens with what you share.

**Your quiz answers.** Your answers stay in your browser while you take the quiz. When you ask for your results, your answers are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, only so its AI can write your results. We do not save your answers. We do not keep them in a database, we do not write them to our logs, and we do not use them for training. Gage and our team never see them. Anthropic handles the data it receives under its own terms and privacy policy, which you can read at anthropic.com.

**Your results.** Your results are shown on your screen only. We don't keep a copy. If you want to keep them, take a screenshot or use the "Copy my results" button.

**Your name and email.** To see your results, you give us your first name and email. Your email is never sent to the AI. After your results are written, we send your first name, your email and, when it is set up, a short tag for your result type (for example, "Quiz - Freezer") to our email service, Kajabi, so we can send you emails from Gage about confidence and dating. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of any email. To ask us to delete your name and email, write to support@confidenceofayes.com.

**When we don't keep your email.** If you tell us you are under 18, or if your answers suggest you may be in crisis or may hurt someone, we don't show sales offers and we don't send your name or email to Kajabi.

**Technical info.** Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) processes basic technical info, such as your IP address, to deliver the page and keep it safe. Our quiz keeps your IP address in server memory for about an hour, only to stop people from overusing it. We don't save it in any database. Vercel's own request logs may include it for a limited time under Vercel's privacy policy. We don't use ads, tracking pixels or session recording on the quiz.

**AI results can be wrong.** The results are written by AI and are for general information only. They are not therapy, counseling or medical advice.

**Age.** The quiz is for people 18 and older. If you're under 18, please don't take it.

**If you're struggling.** If you feel hopeless or think about hurting yourself, call or text 988 in the U.S. If you are in danger right now, call 911.
